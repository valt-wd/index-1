Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers regarding appointment protocols, visiting regulations, insurance processing, and medical records.

Most outpatient specialist appointments can be accommodated within 24 to 48 hours. Urgent cardiac, trauma, or neurological assessments are triaged immediately upon arrival through our 24/7 Emergency & Trauma Centre without requiring advance appointment bookings.
In a production hospital deployment, Aurelia Care coordinates with major private insurers, Third Party Administrators (TPAs), and corporate healthcare panels to provide direct cashless billing. Our Patient Concierge Desk assists with pre-authorization paperwork prior to planned procedures.
For private inpatient suites, visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM and 04:30 PM to 08:00 PM daily. One designated family attendant is permitted 24/7 in private rooms. To safeguard clinical sterility, Intensive Care Unit (ICU) visits are scheduled in 30-minute intervals twice daily.
Diagnostic results, lab panels, and DICOM imaging files are synchronized to our secure Patient Digital Portal within hours of testing. Printed copies and medical CD-ROM packages are also available at the Diagnostic Records Lounge on Ground Floor Level 1.
Please bring a government-issued photo ID, your insurance membership card, a list of current prescription medications, and copies of any relevant medical records, previous surgeries, or recent imaging scans from the past 12 months.
Our main campus is situated at 1400 Grandview Boulevard in Metro Health District (Demo Location). We offer complimentary patient valet parking at Gate 2, as well as a 500-vehicle covered multi-level parking garage with EV charging and wheelchair-accessible elevator bays.
